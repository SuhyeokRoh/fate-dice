This list covers the core "Artwork and Graphics" credits only. 120 of the 338 Wesnoth images used here are
campaign portraits from 15 mainline campaigns; Wesnoth credits campaign-specific artists in each campaign's own
[about] blocks (data/campaigns/<campaign>/_main.cfg), which were not copied here, so some portrait artists may
be missing from the names below. See each campaign's credits in Wesnoth (in-game Credits screen, or the files above):
Descent_Into_Darkness (7), Eastern_Invasion (7), Heir_To_The_Throne (23), Legend_of_Wesmere (8), Liberty (6),
Northern_Rebirth (10), Sceptre_of_Fire (10), Secrets_of_the_Ancients (6), Son_Of_The_Black_Eye (10),
The_Deceivers_Gambit (11), The_Hammer_of_Thursagan (6), The_Rise_Of_Wesnoth (9), The_South_Guard (5),
Two_Brothers (1), Under_the_Burning_Suns (1).
